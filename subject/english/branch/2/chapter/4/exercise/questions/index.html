Q1

1. What is the emotional impact of the refrain, 'I cannot remember my mother'?

1. Answer: The repeated line touches the reader's heart with a quiet, wistful feeling. While it reminds us of the sad truth that the poet lost his mother when he was too young to remember her face, each time the line appears it is followed by a warm memory through sound, smell, or sight—showing that a mother's love is never really lost.

Q2

2. Interpret the connection between the poet's mother and the following:
(i) shiuli flowers (ii) humming tune

  • (i) Shiuli flowers: In autumn mornings, the sweet fragrance of shiuli flowers used in temple worship blends with the scent of the morning service, reminding the poet of his mother's gentle, pure, and spiritual presence.
  • (ii) Humming tune: While absorbed in play, a faint melody seems to linger over his toys, reconnecting him to the soothing lullabies his mother used to hum while rocking his cradle.
Q3

3. What role does nature play in the poet's description of the memory of his mother?

3. Answer: Nature acts as a gentle keeper of his mother's memory. Through the autumn breeze carrying the fragrance of shiuli flowers and the wide, peaceful blue sky looking down on him, nature keeps his mother's loving presence alive all around him.

Q4

4. What can be inferred about the poet's perception of the mother-child relationship?

4. Answer: We can see that the poet views the bond between a mother and her child as timeless and unbreakable, reaching far beyond physical absence. Even when a child cannot picture his mother's face, her love stays alive in his heart and in the everyday world around him.
