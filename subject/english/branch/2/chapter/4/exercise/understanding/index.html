Q1

I Fill in the blanks with appropriate words.

Stanza 1
1. The poet remembers his mother while he ________________.
2. The poet remembers the ________________ but not the ________________.
Setting: ________________ (outdoor/indoor)
Stanza 2
1. The poet remembers his mother in the ________________ season.
2. The poet remembers his mother by the smell of ________________.
Setting: ________________ (outdoor/indoor)
Stanza 3
1. The poet feels that his mother ________________ at him from the ________________.
Setting: ________________ (outdoor/indoor)
  • Stanza 1:
    1. is playing / plays
    2. tune; words / song
    Setting: indoor
  • Stanza 2:
    1. autumn
    2. shiuli flowers (and the morning service in the temple)
    Setting: outdoor
  • Stanza 3:
    1. gazes / looks; sky
    Setting: indoor (looking out from his bedroom window)
Q2

II The senses of olfactory (smell), auditory (hearing), and visual (sight), are three of the five basic senses in humans. Give examples of references to senses from the poem. Complete the table given below.

Stanza Lines from the Poem Senses
1. ...the tune of some song that she used to hum while rocking my cradle ________________
3. ________________ ________________
2. ________________ olfactory
  • Stanza 1:
    • Lines: "...the tune of some song that she used to hum while rocking my cradle"
    • Sense: auditory (hearing)
  • Stanza 3:
    • Lines: "...I send my eyes into the blue of the distant sky, / I feel that the stillness of my mother's gaze on my face has spread all over the sky."
    • Sense: visual (sight)
  • Stanza 2:
    • Lines: "...the smell of the shiuli flowers floats in the air / the scent of the morning service in the temple comes to me as the scent of my mother."
    • Sense: olfactory (smell)
Q3

III Read the poem silently once again and complete the following.

  1. Two examples of alliteration from the poem are (i) ________________ and (ii) ________________.
  2. An example of onomatopoeia is ________________.
  3. The poem uses imagery extensively. Explain.
  4. Although the poem does not have a rhyme scheme, it is enjoyable because ________________.
  5. What is the tone of the poet? Why do you say so?
  6. What impact does the title of the poem have on the overall mood of the poem?
  7. The poet uses 'I cannot remember my mother' as a refrain because ________________.
  8. The poet uses symbolism to indicate the memory of his mother's presence. Identify the symbols used in the poem.
  1. Alliteration: (i) some song that she and (ii) smell of the shiuli.
  2. Onomatopoeia: hum.
  3. Imagery: The poet uses gentle sights, sounds, and smells—a lullaby lingering over toys, the sweet scent of autumn shiuli flowers and morning temple worship, and the quiet blue sky—to make us feel how his mother's love still surrounds him.
  4. Free Verse Appeal: it flows with a soft, natural rhythm, honest emotion, and warm sensory details that speak straight to the reader's heart.
  5. Tone: The tone is nostalgic, tender, and peaceful because the speaker lovingly notices quiet reminders of his mother in everyday life instead of speaking with bitter sorrow.
  6. Impact of Title: The title immediately creates a gentle, wistful, and touching mood by sharing the speaker's early loss and quiet longing for his mother.
  7. Refrain: it highlights the contrast between not being able to picture his mother's face and yet feeling her presence so strongly through his senses in every stanza.
  8. Symbols: The hovering tune stands for a mother's soothing warmth; the shiuli flowers and morning temple service stand for purity and sacred love; and the distant blue sky stands for his mother's endless, watchful care over him.
