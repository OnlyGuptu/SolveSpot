Chapter 4: Vitamin-M — Complete Questions and Answers (14 Q&As)

Q1
  1. Why did Grandpa dislike living in the city with his daughter?
  2. Why did Vidya not want her father to go out alone?
  3. Was Ravi in favour of his mother treating Grandpa like a child? How can you say so?
  4. Would Ravi be able to keep up his promise to his mother?
  1. I Answer: Grandpa hated the noise and rush of city life, felt cramped in a "poky little flat", and missed his quiet brick house in his hometown with its big mango tree and peaceful garden.
  2. II Answer: Vidya was worried about his safety because the previous month he had accidentally taken a double dose of medicine, lost his way on a walk, and earlier slipped in his garden and lay outside all night with no one to help him.
  3. III Answer: No, Ravi did not like it. We can tell because he winced whenever his mother spoke to Grandpa in a loud voice, as if he were a small child who could neither hear nor understand properly.
  4. IV Answer: Yes, Ravi keeps his promise to make sure Grandpa is safe, though he has to secretly follow him all around town and go through several funny mix-ups along the way.
Q2

I Complete the flow chart by describing Grandpa's day out.

  • Grandpa first stops at 1. ________________
  • buys 2. ________________ and watches ________________.
  • Grandpa next goes to 3. ________________
  • Ravi gets scolded by 4. ________________ and ________________.
  • Grandpa drinks 5. ________________ and eats ________________.
  • Grandpa crosses the road and enters a 6. ________________
  • Next Grandpa walks quickly to a 7. ________________ and ________________.
  • 1. children's park
  • 2. a paper-cone of peanuts; watches the children play
  • 3. the tea stall
  • 4. a little boy's mother and street vendors
  • 5. sugary tea; eats two bananas and an ice cream
  • 6. barber shop
  • 7. bus stop and boards a bus (after gifting his yellow cap to a stranger)
Q3
  1. Was Grandpa lost as feared by Ravi's mother?
  2. How would Ravi feel after seeing Grandpa?
  3. Do you think Ravi's mother will get to know about what had happened?
  1. II Answer: No, Grandpa was not lost at all. He found his way back home easily and was fast asleep in his bed.
  2. III Answer: Ravi felt a huge sense of relief, happiness, and love when he saw his grandfather sleeping safely at home.
  3. IV Answer: No, she will not find out because Ravi kept quiet so he wouldn't have to admit he had followed Grandpa, and Grandpa playfully teased Ravi about disappearing instead.
Q4
  1. Describe how Ravi's emotional reaction upon finding Grandpa safe at home reveals his feelings towards his grandfather.
  2. What might be the significance of Grandpa's habit of giving gifts to everyone on his birthday rather than receiving them?
  3. Why do you think Grandpa gave Ravi a detective story book as a gift? What might this suggest about Grandpa's awareness of Ravi's actions earlier in the day?
  1. I Answer: Ravi rests his cheek against Grandpa's face and hugs him warmly. This shows that despite all the running around and worry, Ravi deeply loves and cares for his grandfather.
  2. II Answer: It shows his generous nature and fatherly affection; as the head of the family, he still sees his grown-up children and grandson as little ones whom he loves to pamper.
  3. III Answer: Giving Ravi The Best Detective Stories and pointing out that it had tips on "how to avoid getting fooled when one is trailing a suspect" shows that Grandpa knew all along that Ravi had been following him!
Q5

I Read the extracts given below and answer the questions that follow.

1. Grandpa hated the noise and bustle of city life and when they were on their own, he often spoke longingly of his small brick house in town. "Such a wonderful place...! With that big mango tree in the garden! It is so quiet that at dusk you can even hear a leaf fall!" But then Grandpa had slipped and fallen in the garden late one evening while he was pottering around and lain outside all night because there was no one at home to help him up.

  • (i) Choose the emotion displayed by the Grandfather.
    A. regretful
    B. apologetic
    C. nostalgic
    D. wistful
  • (ii) Complete the following sentence with a reason.
    Grandpa hated the busy and noisy city life because ________________.
  • (iii) Why did Grandpa come to the city despite his dislike for city life?
  • (iv) Choose an expression similar in meaning to, 'you can even hear a leaf fall'.
    A. end of one's life
    B. arrival of autumn
    C. sign of good luck
    D. pin drop silence
  • (v) State one advantage and one disadvantage of the Grandfather living in the house in town.
  • (i) Answer: D. wistful (or C. nostalgic)
  • (ii) Answer: he was used to the quiet surroundings, open garden, and freedom of his own house in town.
  • (iii) Answer: After he slipped in his garden one evening and lay outside all night with no one at home to help him, his daughter locked up the house and brought him to the city so she could look after him.
  • (iv) Answer: D. pin drop silence
  • (v) Answer:
    • Advantage: Peaceful, green surroundings and the freedom to live on his own terms.
    • Disadvantage: Nobody nearby to help in case of a fall or medical emergency.
Q6

2. Read the extract given below and answer the questions that follow.

"I had a quiet morning, but I don't know about Ravi. He just disappeared instead of staying at home to look after me like you told him to," answered Grandpa coolly, while Ravi just looked confused and embarrassed.
Grandpa had another surprise for Ravi. A gift-wrapped parcel!
"But, Papa, Ravi's birthday was three months ago. Have you forgotten?" exclaimed Ravi's mother, impatiently.

  • (i) Choose the option that displays the tone of the Grandfather's response.
    A. apologetic
    B. hurtful
    C. calm
    D. puzzled
  • (ii) Why was Ravi confused and embarrassed?
  • (iii) Complete the sentence with a suitable reason.
    Ravi's mother was impatient because ________________.
  • (iv) State whether the following sentence is true or false.
    Grandfather forgot that Ravi's birthday was three months ago.
  • (v) Why did Grandpa say he didn't know what kind of morning Ravi might have had?
  • (i) Answer: C. calm
  • (ii) Answer: He felt confused and embarrassed because he could not explain where he had been without admitting that he had secretly followed Grandpa.
  • (iii) Answer: she thought Grandpa had forgotten the date again and mistaken the day for Ravi's birthday.
  • (iv) Answer: False
  • (v) Answer: He was playfully teasing Ravi, as he knew very well what a tiring and chaotic morning Ravi had spent chasing after him!
Q7

1. Grandpa is portrayed as a person with failing memory in the beginning of the story. Give two evidences to disprove the statement.

1. Answer: First, he remembered difficult chess moves made years ago by famous grandmasters like Karpov and Bobby Fischer. Second, he remembered that it was his own birthday and followed his habit of buying gifts for the family, whereas his daughter completely forgot!

Q8

2. Give two characteristic traits of each of the following characters: (i) Grandfather, (ii) Ravi, (iii) Ravi's mother.

  • (i) Grandfather: Independent, witty, generous, and sharp-minded.
  • (ii) Ravi: Caring, obedient, observant, and affectionate.
  • (iii) Ravi's mother: Worried, protective, caring, but overly strict.
Q9

3. Why was Ravi worried about what Grandpa had at the tea stall?

3. Answer: Grandpa had sweet tea, two bananas, and an ice cream—all of which were strictly forbidden for him at home because of his health. Ravi was afraid this would make him sick and worried about how his mother would react.

Q10

4. Do you think it was easy for Ravi to follow his Grandfather? If yes, why? If no, why not?

4. Answer: No, it was not easy at all. Ravi was scolded by an angry mother in the park, shouted at by street vendors near the banyan tree, chased out of a ladies' beauty parlour, and tricked into boarding a crowded bus.

Q11

5. 'Ravi is a good detective'. Give one argument in favour of and against the given statement.

  • In favour: He was very dedicated, thought quickly on his feet, and ran all across town without giving up on his mission.
  • Against: He gave himself away several times, drew the attention of strangers, and was easily fooled when Grandpa gave his yellow cap to another man.
Q12

6. Was Grandfather aware that Ravi was following him? How do you know?

6. Answer: Yes, he knew all along. He cleverly gave his yellow cap to a stranger in the barber shop so Ravi would follow the wrong person, and later gifted Ravi a book of detective stories while joking about how not to get fooled when following a suspect.

Q13

7. In your opinion, who needs Vitamin-M? And why?

7. Answer: Ironically, Ravi's mother needed 'Vitamin-M' (for memory) the most! While she kept worrying that Grandpa was becoming forgetful, she was the one who forgot her own father's birthday even after circling the date in red on the calendar.

Q14

8. Some people believe society shows bias based on age (being young or old). Give an example where you might have experienced or observed such discrimination, when opinions were disregarded because of age.

8. Answer: In many homes, elderly grandparents are treated like small children and are not allowed to go out or make decisions on their own. Similarly, teenagers and young children are often told they are "too young to understand" when they try to share their views in family matters.
