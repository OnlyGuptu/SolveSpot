Q1

Based on the IUCN Red List stacked column chart:
(i) What does 14,234 represent?
(ii) Approx how many reptile species in 2016?
(iii) Which class(es) had a small increase between 2007 and 2019?

Stacked Column Chart: Number of animal species in the IUCN Red List by class (2007–2019)

Answer:
(i) It shows the total number of endangered animal species across all the listed classes in 2019.
(ii) In the 2016 column, the maroon section (Reptiles) starts just above 1,000 and ends near 2,000, so there were roughly 1,000 endangered reptile species.
(iii) 'Mammals' (dark blue bar at the bottom) and 'Birds' (light blue bar) had only a small increase between 2007 and 2019.
