Q1

Write a linear equation in two variables in which $a = 3$, $b = 0$ and $c = -\frac{1}{5}$.

Answer: The standard form of a linear equation in two variables is $ax + by + c = 0$.
Putting $a = 3$, $b = 0$, and $c = -\frac{1}{5}$, we get:
$3x + 0y - \frac{1}{5} = 0$, which simplifies to $3x - \frac{1}{5} = 0$ (or $15x - 1 = 0$ after multiplying through by 5).

Q3


(i) The cost of a notebook is twice the cost of a pen. Consider the cost of a notebook to be ₹t and that of a pen to be ₹p. Charlie wrote $t = 2p$ whereas Meera wrote $p = 2t$. Which of these two representations is correct?
(ii) In a one-day International Cricket match... two Indian batsmen together scored 176 runs. Manisha expressed this situation as $x + y = 176$, where the number of runs scored by one batsman is x, and the number of runs scored by the other is y. Is this a correct representation?

Answer:
(i) Charlie's equation, $t = 2p$, is right. Since one notebook ($t$) costs twice as much as one pen ($p$), we have to double the price of the pen to get the price of the notebook.
(ii) Yes, Manisha's equation is right. Adding the runs scored by the first batsman ($x$) and the second batsman ($y$) gives their combined score of 176 runs.

Q1

Verify if the ordered pair $(4, 3)$ is a solution of $5x - 6y = 2$. Explain your reasoning.

Answer: Put $x = 4$ and $y = 3$ into the equation $5x - 6y = 2$:
LHS = $5(4) - 6(3) = 20 - 18 = 2$.
RHS = $2$.
Since LHS = RHS, $(4, 3)$ is a solution of $5x - 6y = 2$.

Q2

Find any two solutions for each of the following equations:
(i) $7x - 3y = 21$
(ii) $2x + 3y = 5$

Answer:
(i) $7x - 3y = 21$
Put $x = 0$: $7(0) - 3y = 21 \implies -3y = 21 \implies y = -7$. First solution: $(0, -7)$.
Put $y = 0$: $7x - 3(0) = 21 \implies 7x = 21 \implies x = 3$. Second solution: $(3, 0)$.

(ii) $2x + 3y = 5$
Put $x = 1$: $2(1) + 3y = 5 \implies 3y = 3 \implies y = 1$. First solution: $(1, 1)$.
Put $y = 3$: $2x + 3(3) = 5 \implies 2x = -4 \implies x = -2$. Second solution: $(-2, 3)$.
(Any other pair of values satisfying the equation is also valid.)

Q3

In the equations given below, m and n are unknown constants:
$2mx + 3y = 7$
$4x + ny = -10$
If $(2, 1)$ is the solution of both equations, find the values of m and n.

Answer: Since $(2, 1)$ is a solution to both equations, we put $x = 2$ and $y = 1$ into each:
In the first equation: $2m(2) + 3(1) = 7 \implies 4m + 3 = 7 \implies 4m = 4 \implies \mathbf{m = 1}$.
In the second equation: $4(2) + n(1) = -10 \implies 8 + n = -10 \implies \mathbf{n = -18}$.

Q5

Consider the graph of the equation $3x - 7y = 21$ shown below. Does the point C $(2, 3)$ lie on the line? Does it satisfy the equation? Can points that do not lie on the line satisfy the equation?

Answer:
- From the graph, point C $(2, 3)$ lies above the green line, so it does not lie on the line.
- Checking in the equation $3x - 7y = 21$ with $x = 2$ and $y = 3$: LHS = $3(2) - 7(3) = 6 - 21 = -15 \neq 21$. So it does not satisfy the equation.
- No, a point off the line can never satisfy the equation, because the line is made up of all the points (and only those points) whose coordinates satisfy the equation.

Q1

The following diagrams represent ski hills. Rank the hills in order of their steepness, from least to greatest.

  • Hill A: Rise = 60m, Run = 70m
  • Hill B: Rise = 60m, Run = 110m
  • Hill C: Rise = 80m, Run = 100m
Ski Hills Comparison: Hill A (Slope ≈ 0.857), Hill B (Slope ≈ 0.545), Hill C (Slope = 0.800)

Answer: We compare steepness by finding the slope ($\frac{\text{Rise}}{\text{Run}}$) of each hill:
Slope of Hill A = $\frac{60}{70} \approx 0.857$
Slope of Hill B = $\frac{60}{110} \approx 0.545$
Slope of Hill C = $\frac{80}{100} = 0.800$
From least steep to steepest: Hill B, Hill C, Hill A.

Q2

The ramp at a loading dock rises 2.5 metres over a run of 4 metres. Find the slope of the ramp.

Answer: Slope of the ramp = $\frac{\text{Rise}}{\text{Run}} = \frac{2.5}{4} = \mathbf{0.625}$ (or $\frac{5}{8}$).

Q4

An accessibility ramp for wheelchairs is to be made alongside the staircase. The guidelines given for the slope of the ramp is 1 cm vertical rise to 12 cm horizontal length. What should be the horizontal length of the ramp if the total height of the stairs is 18 cm?

Answer: Given slope = $\frac{\text{Rise}}{\text{Run}} = \frac{1}{12}$, and total vertical rise = $18\text{ cm}$.
$\frac{18}{\text{Run}} = \frac{1}{12} \implies \text{Run} = 18 \times 12 = 216\text{ cm}$.
So the horizontal length of the ramp should be 216 cm (or $2.16\text{ m}$).

Q1

Given below are some everyday situations. Construct a pair of linear equations in two variables for each situation:
Let the cost of one movie ticket be ₹x and the cost of one snack box be ₹y.
(i) On the first day, the family buys 2 movie tickets and 3 snack boxes for ₹850.
(ii) On the second day, the family buys 4 movie tickets and 1 snack box for ₹1100.

Answer:
(i) First day: $2x + 3y = 850$
(ii) Second day: $4x + y = 1100$

Q2

Let the fixed charge for one taxi trip be ₹x and the additional charge per kilometre be ₹y.
(i) Sahil travelled 6 km and paid a total fare of ₹122.
(ii) Meena travelled 8 km and paid a total fare of ₹160.

Answer:
(i) Sahil's trip (fixed charge + 6 km): $x + 6y = 122$
(ii) Meena's trip (fixed charge + 8 km): $x + 8y = 160$

Q3

In a sports meet, tickets for adults cost ₹150 each and tickets for children cost ₹100 each. A total of 200 people attended the meet, and the total amount collected from ticket sales was ₹25,000. (Let the number of adult tickets sold be x and the number of children's tickets sold be y).

Answer:
Total number of people: $x + y = 200$
Total money collected: $150x + 100y = 25000$

Q1(i)

Form a pair of linear equations for each of the following problems and find their solutions.
(i) The sum of two integers is +5 and their difference is -21. Find the two numbers.

Answer:
Let the two integers be $x$ and $y$.
$x + y = 5$   ...(1)
$x - y = -21$   ...(2)
Adding (1) and (2): $2x = -16 \implies x = -8$.
Putting $x = -8$ in (1): $-8 + y = 5 \implies y = 13$.
The two integers are $-8$ and $13$.

Q1(ii)

(ii) The difference between two numbers is 26 and one number is three times the other. Find the numbers.

Answer:
Let the larger number be $x$ and the smaller number be $y$.
$x - y = 26$   ...(1)
$x = 3y$   ...(2)
Putting $x = 3y$ into (1): $3y - y = 26 \implies 2y = 26 \implies y = 13$.
From (2): $x = 3(13) = 39$.
The numbers are $39$ and $13$.

Q1(iv)

(iv) Taxi charges consist of a fixed charge plus a per km charge. For 10 km, paid ₹155. For 15 km, paid ₹220. Find fixed charge and charge per km. Cost for 25 km?

Answer:
Let the fixed charge be ₹$x$ and the charge per km be ₹$y$.
$x + 10y = 155$   ...(1)
$x + 15y = 220$   ...(2)
Subtracting (1) from (2): $5y = 65 \implies y = 13$ (charge per km = ₹13).
Putting $y = 13$ in (1): $x + 10(13) = 155 \implies x = 155 - 130 = 25$ (fixed charge = ₹25).
Fare for $25\text{ km} = x + 25y = 25 + 25(13) = 25 + 325 =$ ₹350.

Q1(vii)

(vii) Five years ago, Nuri was thrice as old as Sonu. Ten years later, Nuri will be twice as old as Sonu. How old are Nuri and Sonu?

Answer:
Let Nuri's present age be $N$ years and Sonu's present age be $S$ years.
Five years ago: $N - 5 = 3(S - 5) \implies N - 3S = -10$   ...(1)
Ten years later: $N + 10 = 2(S + 10) \implies N - 2S = 10$   ...(2)
Subtracting (1) from (2): $(N - 2S) - (N - 3S) = 10 - (-10) \implies S = 20$.
Putting $S = 20$ in (2): $N - 2(20) = 10 \implies N = 50$.
Nuri is 50 years old and Sonu is 20 years old.

Q3

Use the ratios $\frac{a_1}{a_2}, \frac{b_1}{b_2}, \frac{c_1}{c_2}$ to determine whether the lines representing the following pairs of linear equations intersect at a point, are parallel or are coincident.
(i) $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$; $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$
(ii) $9x + 3y + 12 = 0$; $18x + 6y + 24 = 0$
(iii) $6x - 3y + 10 = 0$; $2x - y + 9 = 0$

Answer:
(i) $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{5}{7}$ and $\frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-4}{6} = -\frac{2}{3}$. Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$, the lines intersect at a single point (unique solution).
(ii) $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{9}{18} = \frac{1}{2}$, $\frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$, and $\frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{12}{24} = \frac{1}{2}$. Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$, the lines are coincident (infinitely many solutions).
(iii) $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3$, $\frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-3}{-1} = 3$, and $\frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{10}{9}$. Since $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$, the lines are parallel (no solution).

Geometric interpretation of linear systems: Intersecting, Coincident, and Parallel lines
Q8

5 pencils and 7 pens together cost ₹50, whereas 7 pencils and 5 pens together cost ₹46. Find the cost of each pencil and pen.

Answer:
Let the cost of 1 pencil be ₹$x$ and 1 pen be ₹$y$.
$5x + 7y = 50$   ...(1)
$7x + 5y = 46$   ...(2)
Adding (1) and (2): $12x + 12y = 96 \implies x + y = 8$   ...(3)
Subtracting (2) from (1): $-2x + 2y = 4 \implies -x + y = 2$   ...(4)
Adding (3) and (4): $2y = 10 \implies y = 5$.
Putting $y = 5$ in (3): $x + 5 = 8 \implies x = 3$.
Cost of one pencil = ₹3, Cost of one pen = ₹5.

Q1

The graph of the line $y=3x$ passing through $(0, 0)$ and $(2, 6)$ is given below.
(i) Identify the slope of the line from the graph and explain how you calculated it.
(ii) What is the y-intercept of the line? Explain its significance.
(iv) Without drawing a new graph, determine whether the point $(4, 10)$ lies on this line. Justify your answer.

Graph of the line y = 3x passing through (0, 0) and (2, 6) with Slope = 6/2 = 3

Answer:
(i) Slope = $\frac{\text{Rise}}{\text{Run}} = \frac{6 - 0}{2 - 0} = \frac{6}{2} = \mathbf{3}$.
(ii) Since the line passes through the origin $(0,0)$, the $y$-intercept is $0$. This tells us that when $x = 0$, the value of $y$ is also $0$.
(iv) No. Putting $x = 4$ into $y = 3x$ gives $y = 3(4) = 12 \neq 10$. So the point $(4, 10)$ does not lie on the line.

Q2

The formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit is $F = \frac{9}{5}C + 32$.
(ii) If the temperature is $30^\circ C$, what is the temperature in Fahrenheit?
(iii) If the temperature is $95^\circ F$, what is the temperature in Celsius?
(v) Is there a temperature which is numerically the same in both Fahrenheit and Celsius? If yes, find it.

Answer:
(ii) At $C = 30^\circ\text{C}$: $F = \frac{9}{5}(30) + 32 = 9(6) + 32 = 54 + 32 = \mathbf{86^\circ\text{F}}$.
(iii) At $F = 95^\circ\text{F}$: $95 = \frac{9}{5}C + 32 \implies 63 = \frac{9}{5}C \implies C = \frac{63 \times 5}{9} = 7 \times 5 = \mathbf{35^\circ\text{C}}$.
(v) Yes. Let $F = C = x$:
$x = \frac{9}{5}x + 32 \implies x - \frac{9}{5}x = 32 \implies -\frac{4}{5}x = 32 \implies x = -\frac{32 \times 5}{4} = \mathbf{-40}$.
So $-40^\circ\text{C}$ and $-40^\circ\text{F}$ are numerically equal.

Q5

Give a formula to find the x-intercept of the line $y = mx + c$.

Answer: At the $x$-intercept, the line crosses the $x$-axis, so $y = 0$.
Putting $y = 0$ in $y = mx + c$: $0 = mx + c \implies mx = -c \implies x = -\frac{c}{m}$.
So the $x$-intercept is $-\frac{c}{m}$ (for $m \neq 0$).

Q6

Plan A: ₹50 monthly fee + ₹0.20 per minute. Plan B: ₹30 monthly fee + ₹0.30 per minute.
Find the number of minutes at which both plans cost the same.

Answer: Let the number of minutes be $x$.
Cost of Plan A = $50 + 0.20x$
Cost of Plan B = $30 + 0.30x$
Setting the two costs equal: $50 + 0.20x = 30 + 0.30x \implies 20 = 0.10x \implies x = \frac{20}{0.10} = 200$.
Both plans cost the same at 200 minutes.

Q8

For what values of $p$ does the pair of equations $4x + py + 8 = 0$ and $2x + 2y + 2 = 0$ have a unique solution?

Answer: A pair of linear equations has a unique solution when $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$.
Here, $\frac{4}{2} \neq \frac{p}{2} \implies p \neq 4$.
So the pair of equations has a unique solution for all values of $p$ except $p = 4$.

Q10

Find the value of 'k' for which the following system of equations represents a pair of coincident lines:
$x + 2y = 3$; $(k-1)x + (k+1)y = k+3$.

Answer: Two lines are coincident when $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$.
$\frac{1}{k-1} = \frac{2}{k+1} = \frac{3}{k+3}$
From the first two ratios: $\frac{1}{k-1} = \frac{2}{k+1} \implies k + 1 = 2(k - 1) \implies k + 1 = 2k - 2 \implies k = 3$.
Checking in the third ratio: $\frac{2}{3+1} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{3}{3+3} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$, which matches.
Hence, $k = 3$.
