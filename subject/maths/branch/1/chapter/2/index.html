Q1

Exercise Set 2.1

1. Find the degrees of the following polynomials:

(i) 2x² - 5x + 3      (ii) y³ + 2y - 1      (iii) -9      (iv) 4z - 3

  • (i) 2x² - 5x + 3: The highest power of the variable x is 2, so the degree is 2.
  • (ii) y³ + 2y - 1: The highest power of the variable y is 3, so the degree is 3.
  • (iii) -9: This is a non-zero constant polynomial (can be written as -9x⁰). Its degree is 0.
  • (iv) 4z - 3: The highest power of the variable z is 1, so the degree is 1.

Here are examples of polynomials with the specified degrees:

  • Degree 1 (Linear): 5x + 2
  • Degree 2 (Quadratic): 3y² - 4y + 7
  • Degree 3 (Cubic): 2z³ + z² - 8z + 1

Exercise Set 2.1

3. What are the coefficients of x² and x³ in the polynomial x⁴ - 3x³ + 6x² - 2x + 7?

  • The term containing x² is +6x², so the coefficient of x² is 6.
  • The term containing x³ is -3x³, so the coefficient of x³ is -3.

Exercise Set 2.1

4. What is the coefficient of z in the polynomial 4z³ + 5z² - 11?

The polynomial 4z³ + 5z² - 11 does not contain a term with z to the power of 1. It can be written as 4z³ + 5z² + 0z - 11. Therefore, the coefficient of z is 0.

5. What is the constant term of the polynomial 9x³ + 5x² - 8x - 10?

The constant term is the term that does not contain any variable. In the polynomial 9x³ + 5x² - 8x - 10, the constant term is -10.

1. Find the value of the linear polynomial 5x - 3 if:

(i) x = 0      (ii) x = -1      (iii) x = 2

  • (i) If x = 0: 5(0) - 3 = 0 - 3 = -3
  • (ii) If x = -1: 5(-1) - 3 = -5 - 3 = -8
  • (iii) If x = 2: 5(2) - 3 = 10 - 3 = 7

Exercise Set 2.2

2. Find the value of the quadratic polynomial 7s² - 4s + 6 if:

(i) s = 0      (ii) s = -3      (iii) s = 4

  • (i) If s = 0: 7(0)² - 4(0) + 6 = 0 - 0 + 6 = 6
  • (ii) If s = -3: 7(-3)² - 4(-3) + 6 = 7(9) + 12 + 6 = 63 + 12 + 6 = 81
  • (iii) If s = 4: 7(4)² - 4(4) + 6 = 7(16) - 16 + 6 = 112 - 16 + 6 = 102

Exercise Set 2.2

3. The present age of Salil's mother is three times Salil's present age. After 5 years, their ages will add up to 70 years. Find their present ages.

  1. Let Salil's present age be x years.
  2. His mother's present age is 3x years.
  3. After 5 years, Salil's age will be x + 5, and his mother's age will be 3x + 5.
  4. Their sum after 5 years is 70:
    (x + 5) + (3x + 5) = 70
    4x + 10 = 70
    4x = 60
    x = 15
  5. Therefore, Salil's present age is 15 years, and his mother's present age is 45 years (3 × 15).

    Smaller integer: 2 × 21 = 42

Exercise Set 2.2

5. Ruby has 3 times as many two-rupee coins as she has five-rupee coins. If she has a total ₹88, how many coins does she have of each type?

  1. Let the number of five-rupee coins be x.
  2. Then the number of two-rupee coins is 3x.
  3. The total value of the coins is ₹88:
    (Value of five-rupee coins) + (Value of two-rupee coins) = 88
    5(x) + 2(3x) = 88
    5x + 6x = 88
    11x = 88
    x = 8
  4. So, she has 8 five-rupee coins and 24 two-rupee coins (3 × 8).

Exercise Set 2.2

7. If the length of a rectangle is three more than twice its width and its perimeter is 24 cm, what are the dimensions of the rectangle?

  1. Let the width of the rectangle be w cm.
  2. The length is 2w + 3 cm.
  3. The perimeter of a rectangle is 2 × (Length + Width). Given the perimeter is 24 cm:
    2 × ((2w + 3) + w) = 24
    2 × (3w + 3) = 24
    6w + 6 = 24
    6w = 18
    w = 3
  4. The dimensions are:
    Width: 3 cm
    Length: 2(3) + 3 = 9 cm

  • At the end of the 1st month: ₹500 + (1 × ₹150) = ₹650
  • At the end of the 2nd month: ₹500 + (2 × ₹150) = ₹800
  • At the end of the 3rd month: ₹500 + (3 × ₹150) = ₹950, and so on.
  • The linear expression for the amount in the nth month is: 500 + 150n.

  • After 1 hour: 120 - 9 = 111 members
  • After 2 hours: 120 - 2(9) = 120 - 18 = 102 members
  • After 3 hours: 120 - 3(9) = 120 - 27 = 93 members
  • The linear expression for the members remaining at the end of the nth hour is: 120 - 9n.

3. Suppose the length of a rectangle is 13 cm. Find the area if the breadth is (i) 12 cm, (ii) 10 cm, (iii) 8 cm. Find the linear pattern representing the area of the rectangle.

Area of a rectangle = Length × Breadth. Given Length = 13 cm.

  • (i) If breadth is 12 cm: Area = 13 × 12 = 156 cm²
  • (ii) If breadth is 10 cm: Area = 13 × 10 = 130 cm²
  • (iii) If breadth is 8 cm: Area = 13 × 8 = 104 cm²
  • The linear pattern representing the area as a function of the breadth b is: 13b.

4. Suppose the length of a rectangular box is 7 cm and breadth is 11 cm. Find the volume if the height is (i) 5 cm, (ii) 9 cm, (iii) 13 cm. Find the linear pattern representing the volume of the rectangular box.

Volume of a rectangular box = Length × Breadth × Height. Base Area = 7 × 11 = 77 cm².

  • (i) If height is 5 cm: Volume = 77 × 5 = 385 cm³
  • (ii) If height is 9 cm: Volume = 77 × 9 = 693 cm³
  • (iii) If height is 13 cm: Volume = 77 × 13 = 1001 cm³
  • The linear pattern representing the volume as a function of height h is: 77h.

Exercise Set 2.5

1. A learning platform charges a fixed monthly fee and an additional cost per digital learning module accessed. A student observes that when she accessed 10 modules, her bill was ₹400. When she accessed 14 modules, her bill was ₹500. If the monthly bill y depends on the number of modules accessed, x, according to the relation y = ax + b, find the values of a and b.

  1. We have two equations based on the information provided:
    400 = 10a + b
    500 = 14a + b
  2. Subtract the first equation from the second to eliminate b:
    (500 - 400) = (14a + b) - (10a + b)
    100 = 4a
    a = 25
  3. Substitute a = 25 back into the first equation to find b:
    400 = 10(25) + b
    400 = 250 + b
    b = 150
  4. The relationship is y = 25x + 150.

Exercise Set 2.5

2. A gym charges a fixed monthly fee and an additional cost per hour for using the badminton court. A student using the gym observed that when she used the badminton court for 10 hours, her bill was ₹800. When she used it for 15 hours, her bill was ₹1100. If the monthly bill y depends on the hours of the use of the badminton court, x, according to the relation y = ax + b, find the values of a and b.

  1. Establish the two linear equations:
    800 = 10a + b
    1100 = 15a + b
  2. Subtract the first from the second:
    300 = 5a
    a = 60
  3. Substitute a = 60 into the first equation:
    800 = 10(60) + b
    800 = 600 + b
    b = 200
  4. The relationship is y = 60x + 200.

Exercise Set 2.5

3. Consider the relationship between temperature measured in degrees Celsius (°C) and degrees Fahrenheit (°F), which is given by °C = a°F + b. Find a and b, given that ice melts at 0 degrees Celsius and 32 degrees Fahrenheit, and water boils at 100 degrees Celsius and 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

  1. Set up the equations based on the given points:
    0 = 32a + b
    100 = 212a + b
  2. Subtract the first equation from the second:
    100 = 180a
    a = 100 / 180 = 5/9
  3. Substitute a = 5/9 into the first equation:
    0 = 32(5/9) + b
    b = -160/9
  4. The relationship is °C = (5/9)°F - 160/9 (or °C = (5/9)(°F - 32)).

Exercise Set 2.6

1. Draw the graphs of the following sets of lines. In each case, reflect on the role of 'a' and 'b'.

(i) y = 3x - 1, y = 3x, y = 3x + 1

(ii) y = 4x, y = 2x, y = x

(iii) y = -6x, y = -3x, y = -x

(iv) y = 5x, y = -5x

(v) y = -2x - 3, y = -2x, y = 2x + 3

Reflections on the parameters 'a' (slope) and 'b' (y-intercept):

  • (i) y = 3x - 1, y = 3x, y = 3x + 1: Here, the slope a is constant (3), so the three lines are parallel. The y-intercept b changes (-1, 0, 1), shifting the parallel lines vertically up and down the y-axis.
  • (ii) y = 4x, y = 2x, y = x: Here, the y-intercept b is fixed at 0 (all pass through the origin). The slope a changes (4, 2, 1). As a increases, the lines become progressively steeper (closer to the y-axis).
  • (iii) y = -6x, y = -3x, y = -x: All pass through the origin. Since slopes are negative, they represent linear decay. As the magnitude of a increases (from 1 to 6), the lines get steeper in the negative direction.
  • (iv) y = 5x, y = -5x: Both pass through the origin. They have the same steepness but opposite directions; y = 5x represents growth (rising left to right), while y = -5x represents decay (falling left to right). They are reflections of each other across the y-axis.
  • (v) y = -2x - 3, y = -2x, y = 2x + 3: The first two lines (y = -2x - 3 and y = -2x) are parallel to each other with a negative slope, but with different y-intercepts (-3 and 0). The third line (y = 2x + 3) has a positive slope (intersecting the others) and cuts the y-axis at +3.
Role of 'b': Parallel lines with same slope (a = 3) shifted vertically by y-intercept b

6. The digits of a two-digit number differ by 3. If the digits are interchanged, and the resulting number is added to the original number, we get 143. Find both the numbers.

  1. Let the tens digit be t and the units digit be u. The original number is 10t + u.
  2. The interchanged number is 10u + t.
  3. Their sum is 143:
    (10t + u) + (10u + t) = 143
    11t + 11u = 143
    t + u = 13
  4. We are also given that the digits differ by 3, so either t - u = 3 or u - t = 3.
  5. Case 1: t - u = 3
    Adding the equations: (t + u) + (t - u) = 13 + 3 ⇒ 2t = 16 ⇒ t = 8
    If t = 8, then u = 5. The number is 85.
  6. Case 2: u - t = 3
    Adding the equations: (u + t) + (u - t) = 13 + 3 ⇒ 2u = 16 ⇒ u = 8
    If u = 8, then t = 5. The number is 58.
  7. The two possible numbers are 58 and 85.

7. Draw the graph of the following equations, and identify their slopes and y-intercepts. Also, find the coordinates of the points where these lines cut the y-axis.

(i) y = -3x + 4      (ii) 2y = 4x + 7      (iii) 5y = 6x - 10      (iv) 3y = 6x - 11

Are any of the lines parallel?

Convert each to the form y = ax + b:

  • (i) y = -3x + 4:
    Slope = -3, y-intercept = 4, Coordinates = (0, 4).
  • (ii) 2y = 4x + 7 ⇒ y = 2x + 3.5:
    Slope = 2, y-intercept = 3.5, Coordinates = (0, 3.5).
  • (iii) 5y = 6x - 10 ⇒ y = (6/5)x - 2:
    Slope = 6/5 (or 1.2), y-intercept = -2, Coordinates = (0, -2).
  • (iv) 3y = 6x - 11 ⇒ y = 2x - 11/3:
    Slope = 2, y-intercept = -11/3, Coordinates = (0, -11/3).

Parallel lines: Lines (ii) and (iv) both have a slope of 2, so they are parallel to each other.

8. If the temperature of a liquid can be measured in Kelvin units as x K and in Fahrenheit units as y °F, the relation between the two systems of measurement of temperature is given by the linear equation y = 9/5(x - 273) + 32.

(i) Find the temperature of the liquid in Fahrenheit if the temperature of the liquid is 313 K.

(ii) If the temperature is 158 °F. then find the temperature in Kelvin.

  • (i) From Kelvin to Fahrenheit:
    Substitute x = 313 into the equation:
    y = 9/5(313 - 273) + 32
    y = 9/5(40) + 32
    y = 9(8) + 32 = 72 + 32 = 104 °F
  • (ii) From Fahrenheit to Kelvin:
    Substitute y = 158 into the equation:
    158 = 9/5(x - 273) + 32
    126 = 9/5(x - 273)
    126 × (5/9) = x - 273
    70 = x - 273
    x = 70 + 273 = 343 K

9. The work done by a body on the application of a constant force is the product of the constant force and the distance travelled by the body in the direction of the force. Express this in the form of a linear equation in two variables (work w and distance d), and draw its graph by taking the constant force as 3 units. What is the work done when the distance travelled is 2 units? Verify it by plotting it on the graph.

  1. The relationship is Work = Force × distance.
  2. Given the constant force is 3 units, the linear equation is: w = 3d.
  3. When distance d = 2 units, the work done is: w = 3(2) = 6 units.
  4. (To verify on a graph: Plot the line passing through (0,0), (1,3), and (2,6). Reading the y-value at x=2 confirms it is 6).

10. The graph of a linear polynomial p(x) passes through the points (1, 5) and (3, 11).

(i) Find the polynomial p(x).

(ii) Find the coordinates where the graph of p(x) cuts the axes.

(iii) Draw the graph of p(x) and verify your answers.

  1. (i) Find the polynomial p(x):
    Let p(x) = ax + b. We have:
    5 = a(1) + b ⇒ a + b = 5
    11 = a(3) + b ⇒ 3a + b = 11
    Subtracting the first from the second gives 2a = 6 ⇒ a = 3.
    Substitute a = 3 into the first: 3 + b = 5 ⇒ b = 2.
    The polynomial is p(x) = 3x + 2.
  2. (ii) Coordinates where it cuts the axes:
    y-axis intercept (set x = 0): p(0) = 3(0) + 2 = 2. Coordinates: (0, 2).
    x-axis intercept (set p(x) = 0): 0 = 3x + 2 ⇒ x = -2/3. Coordinates: (-2/3, 0).

11. Let p(x) = ax + b and q(x) = cx + d be two linear polynomials such that:

(i) p(0) = 5.

(ii) The polynomial p(x) - q(x) cuts the x-axis at (3, 0).

(iii) The sum p(x) + q(x) is equal to 6x + 4 for all real x.

Find the polynomials p(x) and q(x).

  1. From (i), p(0) = 5 ⇒ a(0) + b = 5 ⇒ b = 5.
  2. From (iii), p(x) + q(x) = (a + c)x + (b + d) = 6x + 4.
    This means a + c = 6 and b + d = 4.
    Since b = 5, then 5 + d = 4 ⇒ d = -1.
  3. From (ii), p(3) - q(3) = 0 ⇒ p(3) = q(3).
    3a + b = 3c + d
    Substitute b = 5 and d = -1:
    3a + 5 = 3c - 1 ⇒ 3a - 3c = -6 ⇒ a - c = -2.
  4. We now have a system for a and c:
    a + c = 6
    a - c = -2
    Adding them: 2a = 4 ⇒ a = 2.
    Substituting back: 2 + c = 6 ⇒ c = 4.
  5. The polynomials are: p(x) = 2x + 5 and q(x) = 4x - 1.

13. Let p(x) = ax + b and q(x) = cx + d be two linear polynomials such that:

(i) The graph of p(x) passes through the points (2, 3) and (6, 11).

(ii) The graph of q(x) passes through the point (4, -1).

(iii) The graph of q(x) is parallel to the graph of p(x).

Find the polynomials p(x) and q(x). Also, find the coordinates of the point where these lines meet the x-axis.

  1. Find p(x):
    3 = 2a + b
    11 = 6a + b
    Subtracting gives 4a = 8 ⇒ a = 2. Substituting back gives b = -1.
    So, p(x) = 2x - 1.
  2. Find q(x):
    Because q(x) is parallel to p(x), its slope c must equal a. So, c = 2.
    q(x) = 2x + d. It passes through (4, -1), so:
    -1 = 2(4) + d ⇒ -1 = 8 + d ⇒ d = -9.
    So, q(x) = 2x - 9.
  3. X-axis intercepts (where y = 0):
    For p(x): 2x - 1 = 0 ⇒ x = 1/2. Coordinates: (1/2, 0).
    For q(x): 2x - 9 = 0 ⇒ x = 9/2. Coordinates: (9/2, 0).

14. What do all linear functions of the form f(x) = ax + a, a > 0, have in common?

Given f(x) = ax + a, where a > 0. We can factor this as f(x) = a(x + 1).

  1. Positive Slope: The slope is a. Since a > 0, all these lines have a positive slope and slant upwards from left to right.
  2. Equal Slope and Y-intercept: When x = 0, f(0) = a. So the y-intercept is (0, a), which lies on the positive y-axis above the origin.
  3. Same X-intercept: To find where the line cuts the x-axis, put f(x) = 0:
    a(x + 1) = 0
    Since a ≠ 0, we get x + 1 = 0 ⇒ x = -1.
    Therefore, all such lines pass through the same fixed point (-1, 0) on the x-axis.
Pencil of Lines: Every member of f(x) = a(x + 1) pivots through (−1, 0) with slope a
